## Joker & Bloom American Mahjong tile sets

The SVG files under `dist/assets/joker-bloom/` are the Garden, Clear, Ink, Blush, Night, and Contrast American Mahjong tile sets by Joker & Bloom.

- Source: https://jokerandbloom.com/free/tiles/
- License: MIT License (included as `dist/assets/joker-bloom/LICENSE`)
- Attribution: `Tiles by Joker & Bloom (jokerandbloom.com)`

The assets are included unchanged. They provide the 44 tile faces needed by the simplified practice application: three suits, winds, dragons, flowers, Joker, and a tile back.
